How the field scored

Lowest total error wins. The consensus forecaster is the average of every submitted forecast, with dates kept to the day someone could actually pick.

Onset error
Absolute day difference, squared.
Lightning error
Absolute hour difference, then the square root. One day is 24 hours.
Precip error
Absolute inch difference, divided by a modified standard deviation: only forecasts below the mean.
